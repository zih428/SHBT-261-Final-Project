\section{Reproducibility Details}
\label{app:reproducibility}

This appendix records the prompt and metric details needed to reproduce the reported runs from the paper alone. All templates use the same system message: \texttt{You are answering TextVQA questions. Return a short final answer only, with no explanation.} The placeholders \texttt{\{question\}}, \texttt{\{ocr\_tokens\}}, and \texttt{\{question\_type\}} are filled from the manifest and prompt builder at run time.

        Metric & Definition & Role in paper \\
        \midrule
        Exact any-match accuracy & A prediction receives credit if its normalized string exactly matches at least one of the ten normalized human answers. & Primary selection and headline metric \\
        Consensus accuracy & $\min(m/3, 1)$, where $m$ is the number of normalized human answers matching the normalized prediction. & Secondary TextVQA-style agreement score \\
        Token F1 / precision / recall & Token-overlap scores between the normalized prediction and the selected normalized reference answer. & Partial-answer diagnostic \\
        BLEU & Corpus n-gram precision with brevity penalty. & Reported for completeness; not used for model selection \\
        METEOR & Harmonic mean of unigram precision and recall with stemming-aware matching. & Secondary lexical-semantic diagnostic \\
        ROUGE-L & Longest-common-subsequence overlap between prediction and reference. & Secondary overlap diagnostic \\
        LLM-as-a-Judge & GPT-4.1 mini assigns each prediction a semantic score in $\{0, 0.5, 1\}$ against the question and all references. & Semantic diagnostic only \\
        Paired bootstrap and McNemar test & Resampling and paired-disagreement tests over the same 5,000 validation examples. & Uncertainty and significance checks for the headline gain \\
